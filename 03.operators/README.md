### 03. Operators in C

In this directory, I practice various topics related to variables, data types, and operators from my university slides and lectures. My goal is to strengthen my understanding of how data is stored, represented, and manipulated in C programming as a beginner.

The topics covered in this directory include:

* **a. Different types of operators in C**, such as:

  * Assignment operators
  * Arithmetic operators
  * Comparison (relational) operators
  * Logical operators

* **b. Practical use of different types of operators in C**

* **c. Pre-increment, post-increment, pre-decrement, and post-decrement operators**, with a focus on their behavior and use cases.

> **Learning Note:**
> These programs are based on my university learning materials and are written as part of my journey to understand variables, data types, operators, and basic data manipulation in C programming.
